We request written permission for a narrowly defined use of Yahoo Finance
materials, as follows.

1. What we are building
Monafy Ltd is developing an open-source research terminal (AGPL-3.0-or-later)
that a user can self-host. We also intend to operate one public instance on
our own domain for demonstration and research. The interface is a
multi-panel market terminal. It is not presented as a Yahoo product, a
Yahoo API, or a Yahoo-affiliated service.

2. What we ask permission to do
Subject to any conditions you require, we ask Yahoo to grant Monafy Ltd a
revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable permission to:

(a) Display, on the Product URL and in screenshots used only to explain that
    product, delayed market information that Yahoo Finance already makes
    publicly available (quotes, historical prices, fundamentals, and related
    reference fields), in our own terminal layout;

(b) Use the words "Yahoo Finance" and "Yahoo" in the nominative sense, and
    to show the Yahoo Finance favicon or an equivalent small source mark in
    the page footer, solely to identify the source of that information;

(c) Link from that footer to https://finance.yahoo.com/ and to Yahoo's
    Terms of Service.

We are not asking to use Yahoo's primary word mark or logo as our product
name, in our company name, or in a domain name.

3. Attribution and non-affiliation (proposed footer)
The footer on every page would read, in substance:

    Data  Yahoo Finance  via  yfinance
    Not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Yahoo.
    Data is subject to Yahoo's terms; this software does not license it.
    Powered by Monafy Ltd · monafy.com

"Yahoo Finance" and "Yahoo's terms" would be links to Yahoo properties.
We will use ™ / ® notices in the form you specify.

4. What we will not do without a further written licence
- Claim any partnership, sponsorship, certification or endorsement by Yahoo
- Put Yahoo brands in advertising, sales decks, or merchandise
- Remove, obscure or alter Yahoo legal notices
- Sell, sublicence or provide a wholesale API whose purpose is to replace
  Yahoo Finance
- Present the service as suitable for order routing or as a substitute for
  an exchange or licensed real-time feed
- Use Yahoo brand features on any other URL than those listed above

5. Access method
The software retrieves data that Yahoo Finance already publishes, through
the independently maintained open-source library "yfinance"
(https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance), which is not affiliated with
Yahoo. We understand that automated collection and redistribution require
Yahoo's express prior permission. That is why we are writing before a
public launch. We will accept rate limits, delayed-only display, a
restricted symbol universe, or a Yahoo-specified official interface if you
prefer that to the current retrieval method.

6. Territory, users, term
- Territory: worldwide website display, company established in the UK
- Users: members of the public visiting the Product URL, plus self-hosted
  copies of the same software (self-hosted copies would carry the same
  attribution; we accept that permission for our public instance does not
  automatically extend to third-party operators)
- Commercial status: the software is free and open source; Monafy Ltd may
  later offer paid operation of a customer's own instance. We are not
  asking to resell Yahoo's data as a feed
- Duration: 24 months or until withdrawn, renewable
- Volume (estimate): [e.g. under N page views/day; delayed quotes only;
  no continuous tick republication on the public site]

7. Third-party content
We understand that much of what appears on Yahoo Finance is licensed from
exchanges and vendors (including LSEG and others), and that Yahoo cannot
grant rights it does not own. We ask you to:
- grant what Yahoo can grant (Yahoo-owned branding, and any Yahoo right in
  the public HTML/JSON display); and
- tell us which underlying licensors we must approach separately for a
  public display terminal.

8. Attachments
- Companies House extract for Monafy Ltd
- Footer mock-up and two screenshots of the terminal (unmodified Yahoo
  word marks; no Yahoo logo lock-up in the chrome)
- One-page product description

We would be grateful for written approval, a short list of required
changes, or a pointer to the correct commercial team if this should be
handled as a display or data partnership rather than a brand permission.

We will not launch the public instance, and we will not use Yahoo brand
features beyond factual nominative reference, until we have your written
position.
